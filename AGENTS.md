# Agent instructions

## Asking the owner questions and decisions

Ask every question and every decision for the owner **one at a time, in a card** (the question dialog, not a paragraph of chat text). Give plentiful context in plain English: what is being asked, why it matters, what each choice would commit the standard to, and what is left open either way. Put your recommendation first and say why. Do not bundle several questions into one message, and do not make the owner read jargon or file contents to understand the choice.
